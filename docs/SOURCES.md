# Sources

All sources are public. Formulas are standard kinematics and probability; worked examples, the DDIL delivery model and the architecture comparison model are the author's.

## Decision cycle

- Boyd, J. R., *Patterns of Conflict* (briefing, 1986) and *The Essence of Winning and Losing* (briefing, 1996).
- Osinga, F. P. B., *Science, Strategy and War: The Strategic Theory of John Boyd*, Routledge, 2007.
- Alberts, D. S. and Hayes, R. E., *Power to the Edge: Command and Control in the Information Age*, CCRP, US Department of Defense, 2003.
- US Army, ADP 6-0, *Mission Command: Command and Control of Army Forces*, 2019.

## Counter-UAS engagement chain and time budget

- US Army, ATP 3-01.81, *Counter-Unmanned Aircraft Systems Operations*, 2025 (detect, track and identify, defeat, report).
- Joint Interagency Task Force 401, *Counter-Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems Quick Reference Guide*, Version 4, cleared for open publication, 2026 (engagement cycle, layered defence, pre-delegated engagement authority, site criteria).
- US Joint Publication 3-01, *Countering Air and Missile Threats*; JP 3-60, *Joint Targeting* (defensive and offensive process chains).

## Raid arithmetic and defence as control

- Washburn, A. and Kress, M., *Combat Modeling*, Springer, 2009 (salvo and engagement probability models).
- Hughes, W. P., "A Salvo Model of Warships in Missile Combat Used to Evaluate Their Staying Power", *Naval Research Logistics* 42 (1995): 267-289.
- Benaskeur, A., "A Control-Theoretic Unification of Defensive and Offensive C2", *Annals of Command and Control* 3(1), NATO Command and Control Centre of Excellence, 2026 (defence as disturbance rejection, the four asymmetries, vulnerability versus capability; research article, not NATO policy).
- Ogata, K., *Modern Control Engineering*, 5th ed., Prentice Hall, 2010 (disturbance rejection, delay and phase margin).

## Distributed control, complexity and DDIL

- Benaskeur, A., "Holonic Architectural Paradigm for Complex Modern C2", *Annals of Command and Control* 3(1), NATO Command and Control Centre of Excellence, 2026 (saturation from total integration, requisite variety, bounded autonomy, reconciliation after partition).
- Ashby, W. R., *An Introduction to Cybernetics*, Chapman and Hall, 1956 (law of requisite variety).
- Cerf, V. et al., RFC 4838, *Delay-Tolerant Networking Architecture*, IETF, 2007 (store and forward across intermittent links).
- Brewer, E., "CAP Twelve Years Later: How the 'Rules' Have Changed", *Computer* 45(2), IEEE, 2012 (consistency versus availability under partition).
- US Department of Defense, *DoD Digital Modernization Strategy*, 2019 (denied, disrupted, intermittent and limited environments).

## What is not sourced

The DDIL presets, per-hop delivery model (`c2latency.ddil`), architecture comparison (`c2latency.architecture`), common-mode illustration and all worked examples are the author's teaching models with assumed values. They do not describe any fielded system.
